Data and methods

AOD is approximate; gray map areas have no usable observation. Detections use equal-size orange markers. Opacity is proportional to FRP, capped at full opacity at 100 MW; unknown power uses 50% opacity. Marker size stays constant on screen while zooming. Legend ranges show representative opacity; the mapping is continuous. Hotspot types are inferred, not confirmed causes. “Vegetation fire” means presumed vegetation fire. Points are detections, not distinct fires. NASA FIRMS annual MODIS records and recent GIBS Terra records are downloaded locally and loaded by day. AOD imagery is loaded live from GIBS. County borders and the base map are grayscale. Histogram counts are statewide and follow type and confidence filters, independently of the map selection.
